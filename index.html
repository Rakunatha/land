Research instrument

Systems-Based Legal Workflow Intelligence for Indian Family Law Practice

This questionnaire supports doctoral/applied research into how a systems-based intelligence platform could support case tracking, drafting, and decision-making in Indian family law practice. It takes about 8–10 minutes. Responses are used for academic research only, will be reported in aggregate, and individual identities will not be disclosed without separate consent.
